## Exam Feedback delta

### Important validations

- raising requires a higher threshold than calling;
- large sizes require value hands with matching investment ceilings;
- blocking common folds can turn weak air into a poor bluff;
- flop raise classes must migrate on the turn;
- equilibrium bluff-catching can require exploitative overfolding when a live pool underbluffs the exact branch;
- extreme overbets may be equilibrium-mandatory for one value tier but not practically mandatory against every pool;
- favourable rivers can eliminate much of the medium-showdown-value checking region;
- strong checks can preserve value through induced bets and check-raises;
- low SPR increases the availability of thin value/denial hybrids;
- OOP value/denial bets need not be favourites when called because checking leaves the action open.
