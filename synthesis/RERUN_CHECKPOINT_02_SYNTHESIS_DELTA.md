## L14 closure delta

### H-R02-001 — Match each bluff to the value region of its exact size

Domain: river aggression  
Tag: `GENERAL_CORE`  
Tier: CORE  
Confidence: high mechanism  
Suggested status: `DRILL_READY`

A no-showdown hand is not automatically assigned to the largest river size. First identify the value region represented by each size, then choose bluffs whose blockers and range ancestry match that bucket.

Cue:

`Which value bucket does this bluff belong beside?`

### H-R02-002 — Shift bluff supply around the turn class that connected

Domain: turn barreling  
Tag: `GENERAL_CORE`  
Tier: SUPPORTING  
Confidence: medium-high  
Suggested status: `DRILL_READY`

When the low part of the flop-overbet range improves, the bluff supply shifts toward middling or high-card hands. When high cards interact, lower irrelevant candidates may remain available. Do not reuse one fixed bluff list across the turn deck.

Cue:

`Which part of my bluff range just connected?`

### H-R02-003 — Wider betting branches generally use smaller sizes

Domain: sizing architecture  
Tag: `GENERAL_CORE`  
Tier: SUPPORTING  
Confidence: high direction  
Suggested status: `DRILL_READY`

When a turn supports broad value and protection betting, the dominant size usually decreases. Narrow, polar value regions support the largest sizes.

Cue:

`Is this branch wide or polar?`

### Existing candidate strengthened — Bluff supply must be seeded before the river

L14 directly confirms that later bluffs must have used the flop overbet. A player who omits difficult flop air becomes much more value-heavy on later streets.

### Existing candidate strengthened — Every turn bluff needs a river job

The lesson adds multiple-size river matching: a candidate must not only reach the river, but also fit the blocker and value architecture of its chosen size.
