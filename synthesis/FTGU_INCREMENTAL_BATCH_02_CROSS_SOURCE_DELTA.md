### 2. Postflop initiative is replaced by branch composition

E19–E21 and E26–E29 repeatedly reject automatic aggression based on who raised preflop.

The executable spectrum is:

`range/position disadvantage → range check`

`moderate/equalised advantage → selective polar split`

`large concentrated advantage → high-frequency small bet`

After any bet-call, the ranges must be recalculated before repeating the strategy on the next street.

Strongest relations:

- H-W01-004 — confirmed: board labels are meaningless without preflop range shape;
- H-W01-005 — strongly confirmed by flop filtering after range bets;
- H-W02-006 — confirmed by probe and turn c-bet composition;
- H-W02-007 — confirmed: exploit the exact check/call/capped branch;
- H-W03-003 — extended: a wide betting range must become more selective after its opponent filters;
- H-W03-004 — strongly confirmed: respect protected checks, attack weak checks;
- H-R05-001 — strongly confirmed: ownership can reverse after filtering;
- H-R05-002 — confirmed and bounded: heavy-check branches need active defence, but not every simplified split requires a large check-raise tree.

### 3. Capped-range aggression gains a size-function split

E20, E21 and E26 align with Smash:

- overbet: strongest unique value plus highest-quality bluffs;
- modest bet: thinner value, protection and weaker bluffs;
- uncapped opponent: narrower, more selective polar aggression.

E26 adds a clean exploit split against severe overfolding:

- bluff and semi-bluff large;
- value bet smaller when a large size would receive no calls.

This is an extension of H-W02-001 and H-W02-003, not a new universal sizing rule.

`VALUE → SIZE EXCLUSIONS → BLUFF ANCESTRY → HERO BLOCKERS → EVIDENCE`

No generic "regs underbluff" or "aggro players overbluff" rule is admitted. The exploit remains node- and evidence-specific.
