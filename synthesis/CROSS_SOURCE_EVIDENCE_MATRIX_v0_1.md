## FTGU intake procedure

For each accepted lesson:

1. add canonical source record;
2. extract source-supported mechanisms;
3. map each mechanism to candidate IDs;
4. assign relation type and assumptions;
5. record contradictions without resolving them prematurely;
6. propose consolidation only after the module is complete.

## Conflict record

A conflict must include:

- exact assumptions from both sources;
- whether rake, stack, position, size or range differs;
- whether one source is baseline and the other exploit;
- whether the difference is strategic or pedagogical;
- proposed resolution status: `REAL_CONFLICT`, `CONTEXT_SPLIT`, `WORDING_ONLY`, or `OPEN`.
