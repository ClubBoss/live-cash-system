## Grade 2 closure effect

Grade 2 lectures materially close or stabilise the general mechanism for:

- polarising versus condensing actions;
- world favourability versus relative polarisation;
- value and bluff tiering;
- frequency versus sizing and raising;
- theoretical, exploitative and erroneous slow-play;
- robust versus frail continuations;
- range geography against bets;
- bluff-catching from origin range and filter density;
- 3-bet-pot flop plan construction;
- postflop raising against merged and polar bet shapes.

They do not close:

- exact depth or straddle bands;
- deep-OOP thresholds;
- squeeze construction;
- polar preflop target folds;
- independent preflop anchors;
- multiway structure;
- target live population magnitude.
