## Boundaries added

- A low-SPR node can support aggressive frequency without requiring a large size.
- A small flop bet can be strategically close to checking while still placing a large defence burden on the caller.
- Range advantage alone does not determine frequency; relative polarisation can reverse the output.
- A larger turn size is most useful against a merged medium-equity continue region.
- A larger size can be harmful when it increases jam frequency against Hero's medium-EV region.
- Low SPR does not remove the need to protect checks.
- In-position callers can need extremely wide small-bet defence while raising almost nothing.
- Reopening at low SPR can destroy EV when the call branch already realises sufficiently.
