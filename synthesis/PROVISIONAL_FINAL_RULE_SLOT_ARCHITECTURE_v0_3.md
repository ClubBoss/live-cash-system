## Current compression hypotheses

- Slot 9 into Slot 8 as a size-eligibility branch;
- parts of Slots 4 and 5 into one `source range -> filter -> ownership` sequence;
- parts of Slots 6 and 7 into one protected-response tree;
- parts of Slots 11 and 12 into one ancestry-and-compensation tree.

### Likely overlay extraction

- Slot 2 as environment translation;
- Slot 14 as opponent/action-arrival overlay;
- population magnitudes under Slot 16 rather than core rules.

### Split candidates

- Slot 3 if open/flat/squeeze/3-bet decisions cannot share one retrievable cue safely;
- Slot 10 if heads-up probe and multiway delayed aggression produce different table questions;
- Slot 13 if multiway defence and multiway aggression require independent retrieval paths.

No split or merge is admitted yet.

## Anti-count rule

Do not:

- preserve a slot because the list should contain sixteen;
- merge two mechanisms to reduce the count;
- split one mechanism to increase coverage appearance;
- treat a provisional slot number as a learner-facing ID;
- use the former 14-18 target in any active authority.

A merge is valid only when one cue retrieves both mechanisms safely across their scopes. A split is valid only when combined retrieval causes material errors or lost EV.
