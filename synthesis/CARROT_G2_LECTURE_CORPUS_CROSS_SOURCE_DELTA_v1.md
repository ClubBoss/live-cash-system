## Course-level contribution

Grade 2 supplies a unified operating model for translating filtered ranges into betting, sizing, raising and bluff-catching decisions.

```text
ACTION FILTER
→ RANGE SHAPE
→ WORLD FAVOURABILITY
→ BET FREQUENCY
→ RELATIVE POLARISATION
→ SIZE / RAISE BREADTH
→ HAND TIER
→ RESPONSE THRESHOLD
→ ANCESTRY / EXPLOIT EVIDENCE
```

The strongest addition is not a new strategic object. It is a more executable ordering of mechanisms already present across Smash, FTGU, Cash Injection and Grade 1.

## Strongest cross-source confirmations

### Filtering and current ownership

Grade 2 strongly confirms:

- `H-W01-005` — every action changes the current range;
- `H-W01-009` — current frequency depends on origin and prior reach;
- `H-R05-001` — ownership must be recalculated after each filter.

Grade 2 adds clean learner-facing language for:

- polarising versus condensing actions;
- favourable, neutral and unfavourable worlds;
- relative rather than absolute polarisation.

### Value, bluff and sizing order

Grade 2 strongly confirms:

- `H-W02-001` — value threshold before bluff volume;
- `H-W02-002` — bluff quality depends on present and future jobs;
- `H-W02-003` — large sizing requires surviving polarisation;
- `H-W02-004` — bet shape determines raise breadth.

The course supplies a compact distinction:

```text
WORLD FAVOURABILITY
→ mainly frequency and bluff selectivity

RELATIVE POLARISATION
→ mainly sizing and raising
```

This is a pedagogical simplification, not a claim that frequency and size are completely independent.

## Context splits and apparent tensions

### Range advantage versus raising frequency

No conflict.

- world favourability can make Hero fold less or bet more;
- raising frequency depends more heavily on the relative polarisation of Hero’s continuing range versus Villain’s betting range.

A globally weak range can still raise a polar top region.

### Strong hand versus slow-play

No conflict.

- IP check can be valuable because it closes action;
- OOP check can induce bets or preserve a check-raise branch;
- blockers and opponent reinvestment determine whether slow-play is theoretical, exploitative or erroneous.

### Small bet versus big raise response

No conflict.

- a small range bet can require wide defence and wider raises;
- a small selective/polar bet can still receive fewer raises;
- price alone does not reveal bet shape.

### Grade 2 population statements

L08 includes practical claims about early-position underbluffing and late-position natural bluff supply. These align with Cash Injection ancestry, but magnitude and Batumi transfer remain field-gated.
