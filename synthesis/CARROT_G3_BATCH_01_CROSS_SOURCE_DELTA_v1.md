## Lecture 01 delta

### New precision

Lecture 1 sharpens the difference between:

- a true near-indifference mix;
- an optional action with negligible EV difference;
- a clearly inferior action;
- uncertainty caused by lack of knowledge.

Only the first two justify randomisation.
