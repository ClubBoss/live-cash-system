## Grade 3 completion delta

Grade 3 did not become the missing preflop/depth/multiway course. Its observed contribution is concentrated in advanced postflop execution:

- pure-versus-mix reasoning;
- blocker-function vectors;
- call/raise threshold separation;
- extreme sizing and investment ceilings;
- triple-barrel bluff classes;
- protected checking ranges;
- OOP 3-bet-pot defence;
- low-SPR four-bet-pot strategy;
- complete advanced assessment and repair layer.

This materially improves Carrot's 3-bet/4-bet postflop and adaptive usefulness. It does not close exact anchors, multiway or deep-stack boundaries.

## Source-value stop decision

No broad additional course ingestion is justified by the current known gaps.

The highest-value next evidence is:

1. independent preflop anchor construction;
2. multiway boundary and drill work;
3. depth/straddle overlays;
4. learner diagnostics;
5. target-live field calibration.

A new source should be added only if it directly addresses a material gap with better evidence than independent work, not because the catalogue feels incomplete.
