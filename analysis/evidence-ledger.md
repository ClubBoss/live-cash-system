# Evidence Ledger

Tracks the support behind material strategic claims and playbook changes.

| Evidence ID | Concept / rule | Evidence type | Source path | Conditions | Supports / challenges | Strength | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence types

- `INSTRUCTOR_CLAIM`
- `SOLVER_OUTPUT`
- `DATABASE_TENDENCY`
- `HAND_EXAMPLE`
- `CROSS_SOURCE_CONSENSUS`
- `EXTERNAL_RESEARCH`
- `PERSONAL_HAND_HISTORY`
- `FIELD_OBSERVATION`
- `DRILL_RESULT`

## Strength guidance

Strength is contextual, not a universal numerical score:

- `LOW` — anecdotal, ambiguous, or poorly matched conditions.
- `MEDIUM` — coherent and relevant but limited or indirect.
- `HIGH` — directly matched, repeated, and methodologically strong.

Field observations are not promoted to population rules without adequate recurrence and context.
