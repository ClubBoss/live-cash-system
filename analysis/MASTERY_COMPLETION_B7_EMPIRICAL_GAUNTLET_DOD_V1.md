# B7 — Empirical Gauntlet & Final Calibration — 10/10 Engineering DoD V1

Status: `ACTIVE / HUMAN_FIELD_EVIDENCE_SEPARATE`

## Mission

Instrument the product so the final learning claims can be tested against unseen/table-state behavior rather than inferred from content coverage. Machine tests may prove the measurement/routing system; they may not fabricate human learning evidence.

## 10/10 engineering definition

1. Performance telemetry records decision ID, skill, mode, response latency, action correctness, reason correctness, combined correctness and confidence.
2. Telemetry remains separate from canonical mastery evidence so analytics loss cannot corrupt progress truth.
3. Perceptual/table-state responses are tagged distinctly from text/mixed responses.
4. Metrics include action accuracy, reason accuracy, combined accuracy, median response latency, hidden-cue accuracy and confidence calibration signal.
5. Changed-node/transfer performance is separately measurable.
6. Boundary performance is separately measurable.
7. A blind First Journey / novice-flow fixture can verify no unseen-concept exam behavior.
8. A transfer fixture can verify non-identical changed-node selection.
9. A table-state fixture can verify hidden-topic perceptual measurement.
10. A retention fixture preserves actual 1/3/7 spacing requirements.
11. Real-hand repair routing remains measurable as a separate field-evidence step.
12. Machine acceptance never claims `HUMAN_MASTERY_VALIDATED`.
13. Human field validation requires at least one real learner/session and unseen transfer observations before empirical closure.
14. Findings from field use should generate bounded repair waves, not reopen the entire curriculum by default.
15. Engineering closes to diminishing returns when further instrumentation would not materially improve the next empirical decision.
