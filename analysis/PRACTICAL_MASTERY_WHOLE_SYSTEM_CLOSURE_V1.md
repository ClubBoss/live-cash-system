## 4. Evidence integrity contract

A single correct item cannot create mastery. Higher evidence requires distinct stimuli:

- recognition: at least 2 distinct correct recognition stimuli;
- direct decisions: at least 3 distinct correct decision stimuli;
- changed/mixed transfer: at least 2 distinct correct transfer stimuli;
- boundary: at least 1 distinct correct boundary stimulus;
- delayed retrieval: separately earned through non-identical retrieval after time;
- real-hand transfer: separately recorded after delayed retrieval.

Repeat-grinding the same stimulus does not inflate distinct evidence.

Integrated practice prioritizes:

1. unresolved mistake families, especially high-confidence misses;
2. due 1/3/7-day retention;
3. changed/mixed transfer and boundaries;
4. underexposed high-EV learned skills.

A normal integrated session targets 8 items and limits repeated use of one skill inside the batch.

## 5. Memory / human-performance contract

Long learner-facing checklists are not the desired end representation unless the task truly requires a sequence.

Preferred transformation:

`environmental habit OR event trigger -> default directional heuristic -> causal why -> amplifiers -> reversals/boundary -> one-variable transfer -> later hidden-cue retrieval`.

The learner should progressively stop consciously reciting the scaffolding. The target is cue-triggered recognition under live-table cognitive load.
