# B3 — Decision-Variation Depth — 10/10 DoD V1

Status: `ACTIVE`

## Mission

Move from a uniform minimum evidence floor to depth proportional to live frequency, mistake cost, transfer difficulty and number of material variables.

The 2 recognition / 3 direct / 2 transfer / 1 boundary ladder remains a minimum honesty floor, not a claim that eight stimuli are sufficient for every skill.

## 10/10 definition

1. Every skill remains protected by the minimum evidence floor.
2. High-frequency/high-cost/high-transfer skills receive additional non-identical transformations.
3. Depth is not expanded for taxonomy symmetry.
4. Extra reps must change a meaningful variable: position, open/bet size, depth, range origin, player behind, action ancestry, board ownership, hand family, blocker, relative position, evidence quality or straddle geometry.
5. `BL-03` receives an explicit second-plus transfer path and is no longer below its own blind DoD.
6. Intensive skills mix semantic and perceptual reps.
7. No exact frequency memorization is introduced merely to create more questions.
8. Repeated prompts must not be paraphrases of the same node.
9. Correct action position remains rotated / non-leaky.
10. Depth targets are ordinal and EV-based, not pseudo-precise solver quotas.
11. Rare/supporting skills stop at the minimum floor unless additional reps have positive net EV.
12. Scheduler can distinguish an intensive family from a minimum-depth family when choosing mixed practice.
13. Extra corpus must remain source-backed under existing assumptions.
14. Diminishing returns are declared when additional variants mostly add surface diversity rather than new causal transfer.
15. B3 ends with a machine-readable rep-depth policy plus targeted expansion for the highest marginal-value families.

## Intensive families

Initial intensive set: `PF-01`, `PF-04`, `PF-06`, `PF-07`, `BL-03`, `BL-04`, `W4-BOARD-01`, `W4-RUNOUT-01`, `OOP-02`, `IP-01`, `3BP-05`, `TURN-02`, `TURN-03`, `RIV-01`, `RIV-03`, `MW-01`, `MW-02`, `DEEP-01`, `DEEP-03`, `EXP-01`.

These are selected because they are frequent, high-transfer, expensive when wrong, or act as reusable perception/decision primitives across later nodes.
