## Mission

Train the high-frequency live-cash situations that differ most from tidy heads-up 100bb trees: multiple ranges, limpers, relative position, deep effective stacks and straddled geometry.

## 10/10 definition

1. Every supported family has recognition, direct decision, changed-node transfer and boundary stimuli.
2. Multiway decisions identify who can still act, who closes action, and which range is sandwiched.
3. Multiway value thresholds become stricter as more ranges continue; one-pair strength is not treated as heads-up equivalent.
4. Nut potential / robust redraws matter more when several ranges can connect strongly.
5. Bluffing is suppressed by multiple continuing ranges unless a source-supported leverage mechanism exists.
6. Limp-heavy preflop branches choose iso/overlimp/fold through fold equity, frequent strength, position and when-called branch — not automatic isolation.
7. Deep-stack learning distinguishes nominal depth from post-action SPR.
8. 150–200bb planning explicitly raises reverse-implied-odds and one-pair overcommitment awareness.
9. Straddles trigger a recomputation of working blind unit, effective depth, action order, position and future SPR.
10. Deep preflop adjustments remain directional unless exact range authority is visually reviewed.
11. The same physical stack may create materially different geometry under a straddle or large preflop action.
12. `MW-05` and `DEEP-02` are visibly unavailable/partial rather than filled with fabricated precision.
13. Exact solver/chart frequencies are not invented.
14. Correct-option positions are not structurally fixed.
15. Remaining A9 gaps after gauntlet are source ceilings or executable validation, not missing supported live mechanisms.
