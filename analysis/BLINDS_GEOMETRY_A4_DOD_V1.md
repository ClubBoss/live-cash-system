## Mission

Build robust blind/geometry instincts without inventing unsupported blind-vs-blind preflop trees.

## 10/10 definition

A4 is 10/10 for all source-supported scope when:

1. BB vs EP/HJ, CO and BTN are separated by origin-range strength rather than one generic defence identity.
2. Open-size sensitivity is explicitly trained.
3. BB price/closing-action logic is connected to rake and realisation.
4. SB vs opens is structurally distinct from BB and accounts for BB behind.
5. Stack asymmetry, straddles, pairwise effective stack and post-action SPR are event-triggered habits/rules rather than a permanent checklist.
6. Blind/depth/rake changes are practiced through one-variable transformations.
7. Postflop BB-vs-SB range-identity differences use SLC-M02-L05 only for what it actually supports.
8. No preflop SB first-in limp/raise, BB-vs-SB-limp or response tree is scored from SLC-M02-L05.
9. Source-blocked BvB-preflop nodes remain inaccessible to mastery and are explicitly reported.
10. No exact visual frequency/range-cell claims are fabricated.
11. Contrast memory hooks exist for BB vs SB, BTN vs EP origin, small vs large open, normal vs straddled geometry.
12. Evidence density is sufficient for supported P0/P1 blind families, or the family is explicitly source-blocked/partial.
13. Learner-facing language explains why the blind identity matters.
14. RU/EN semantics remain aligned.
15. Remaining gaps require new source ingestion/visual verification or are diminishing-return volume.
