# Contradiction Ledger

Records material disagreements without forcing premature resolution.

| ID | Concept | Source A | Source B | Apparent conflict | Different assumptions | Current resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Resolution protocol

1. Confirm that both claims were transcribed accurately.
2. Identify stack depth, positions, sizing, rake, ante/straddle, player count, and opponent model.
3. Determine whether the disagreement is theoretical, exploitative, or merely terminological.
4. Compare EV significance and execution complexity.
5. Select one Batumi-default rule only when the relevant conditions are sufficiently known.
6. Preserve the rejected or conditional alternative with its boundary conditions.

## Status values

- `OPEN`
- `CONTEXT_SPLIT`
- `RESOLVED`
- `DORMANT`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
