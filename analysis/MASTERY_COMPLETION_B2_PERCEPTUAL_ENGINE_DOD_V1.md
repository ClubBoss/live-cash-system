# B2 — Perceptual Poker Engine — 10/10 DoD V1

Status: `ACTIVE`

## Mission

Convert semantic/text competence into rapid recognition of real poker state. The learner must extract the relevant decision node from seats, stacks, straddles, actions, cards and board rather than receiving the topic as a label.

## 10/10 definition

1. Perceptual reps use the same canonical skill IDs and Practical Mastery state/evidence semantics.
2. No parallel mastery system is created.
3. The table state contains material and irrelevant information; the learner must identify the signal.
4. Topic/skill title is hidden until commitment.
5. At least three scaffolding levels exist conceptually: guided, reduced-cue, hidden-cue/no-topic.
6. The first implementation covers representative high-transfer families: effective stack/SPR, BB defence, open-size change, board×range recognition, runout ancestry, 3BP role/board, multiway relative position, straddle geometry, river bluff supply and evidence-qualified live reads.
7. Visual/table metadata includes positions, stack sizes, pot/action history and cards when relevant.
8. The learner answers both action/signal and reason; correctness requires both.
9. Changed-node table states alter one material variable while preserving enough of the original state to expose causality.
10. Perceptual reps are eligible for existing repair and mixed-practice evidence rather than being unscored illustrations.
11. Table-state presentation is usable on mobile and does not require photorealistic art.
12. The UI does not highlight the correct seat/variable in hidden-cue mode before answer.
13. After answer the UI reveals the skill family and explains which visual cue mattered.
14. A learner with no concept exposure is routed to First Journey rather than blind-tested.
15. Further visual polish is deferred once it no longer materially improves recognition/transfer.

## Closure rule

B2 closes to diminishing returns when the architecture proves table-state extraction across representative families and remaining work is primarily art polish or broad stimulus scaling better handled by B3/B4.
