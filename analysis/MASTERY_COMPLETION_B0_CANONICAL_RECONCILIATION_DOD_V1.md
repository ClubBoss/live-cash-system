# B0 — Canonical Coverage Reconciliation — 10/10 DoD V1

Status: `ACTIVE`

## Mission

Reconcile the original Live Cash OS target scope, admitted course/source corpus, the expanded Practical Mastery skill graph, and the current learner evidence model into one canonical coverage ledger.

The objective is not to maximize topic count. The objective is to ensure that every material in-scope concept has an explicit product treatment and that no material skill is silently lost through compression.

## Governing scope

Primary live-cash target remains:
- NLHE 1/3 and 2/5;
- 100–200bb as core depth;
- selected 300–400bb cases where source support exists or can be acquired with positive net EV;
- straddles;
- blind play, especially BB defence and BvB;
- OOP 3-bet pots;
- multiway / limp-heavy live play;
- live population evidence;
- river decisions;
- transfer to real hands.

Out of scope unless independently admitted later: MTT/ICM, PLO, HUD/online operations, bankroll/career management.

## Canonical treatment statuses

Every in-scope source/concept must resolve to exactly one of:

- `CORE_TRAINED` — must be executable at the table and requires an evidence ladder.
- `SUPPORTING_CONCEPT` — must be understood but does not justify an independent mastery tree.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY` — useful lookup/reference material; memorization would have poor learning EV.
- `ADVANCED_CONTEXTUAL` — only trained after prerequisites or in selected live contexts.
- `SOURCE_BLOCKED` — strategically relevant but current answer-key authority is insufficient.
- `LOW_EV_EXCLUDED` — valid information whose incremental training EV is non-positive after cost/risk/maintenance.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — outside the authorized live-cash product target.

## 10/10 definition

B0 is 10/10 only when:

1. The original target-scope checklist is reconciled against the current 86-family Practical Mastery graph.
2. Every originally identified highest-EV practical-fluency gap has an explicit current state.
3. Every current `SOURCE_BLOCKED` / `PARTIAL` family has a source-acquisition decision path.
4. Coverage is evaluated at concept/skill-family level, not by module count or file count.
5. Content depth is evaluated separately from semantic breadth.
6. Perceptual/table-state transfer is evaluated separately from text decision transfer.
7. High-frequency skills are not assumed complete merely because the minimum 2/3/2/1 evidence floor exists.
8. The ledger distinguishes source completeness, teaching completeness, practice depth, transfer depth, retention support and real-hand routing.
9. No legacy 11-module boundary can implicitly classify a missing skill as covered.
10. Course/source materials are allowed to map many-to-one into a robust skill family when that preserves mechanism and transfer.
11. No concept is turned into a separate lesson merely to achieve taxonomy symmetry.
12. Every residual gap receives a net-EV disposition based on expected learning benefit, frequency, mistake cost, transfer leverage, source-acquisition cost, implementation/regression risk and maintenance burden.
13. B1 admission includes **all positive-net-EV residual gaps**, not only P0/P1 or high-EV gaps.
14. Explicitly source-blocked nodes remain fail-closed until authority is admitted.
15. B0 ends with one canonical residual-gap ledger governing B1–B7.

## Net-EV rule

A residual gap enters the completion queue when:

`expected learning/product benefit - source cost - implementation cost - regression risk - maintenance burden > 0`

Priority affects ordering, not eligibility. Any positive-net-EV gap is eligible for closure.

## Closure rule

Continue reconciliation passes while a material in-scope concept lacks a treatment or while a residual gap has an ambiguous disposition. Stop only when all remaining unimplemented items are `LOW_EV_EXCLUDED`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, empirically unresolvable without B7 evidence, or have non-positive marginal EV.
