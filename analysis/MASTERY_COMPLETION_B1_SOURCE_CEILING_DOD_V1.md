# B1 — Source Ceiling Closure — 10/10 DoD V1

Status: `ACTIVE`

## Mission

Close every residual source/content gap whose expected learning/product EV remains positive after source-acquisition cost, implementation/regression risk and maintenance burden.

Priority controls execution order; it does not determine eligibility.

## 10/10 definition

1. Every B0 residual source gap receives a fresh source search/review.
2. Public/free authority is preferred when it is sufficiently scoped and reputable.
3. Solver-derived material may support a skill only when assumptions, stable directional pattern and boundaries are explicit.
4. Exact range cells/frequencies are never inferred from screenshots or copied as universal memory targets.
5. New authority is recorded in `source-authority.ts` with provenance notes.
6. A previously blocked family becomes supported only to the extent the new source actually supports it.
7. If only a bounded mechanism is supported, the skill objective/content is narrowed instead of fabricating a complete tree.
8. Every newly supported practical family receives recognition, direct decision, changed-node and boundary evidence proportional to its EV.
9. `SOURCE_BLOCKED` / `PARTIAL` evidence ceilings are removed only after the corresponding scored content is source-backed.
10. Any residual positive-EV gap that cannot be autonomously closed is labeled `POSITIVE_EV_SOURCE_ACCESS_REQUIRED`, not diminishing returns.
11. Low-EV status is never used merely because source acquisition is inconvenient.
12. All positive-net-EV cheap gaps are closed in this wave.
13. High-cost gaps are pursued when their expected learning benefit still dominates acquisition/maintenance cost.
14. The wave may proceed around isolated inaccessible source nodes; they do not freeze unrelated curriculum branches.
15. Closure ends only when every residual is either supported, positive-EV-but-access-blocked with an explicit next evidence action, or truly non-positive marginal EV.
