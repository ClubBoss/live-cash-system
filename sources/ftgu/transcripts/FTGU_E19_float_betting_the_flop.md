# Source-Faithful Record

Here `float bet` means betting as the in-position preflop caller after the preflop raiser checks, not calling a c-bet.

The lesson warns against assuming every check is surrender. In theory, an out-of-position preflop raiser protects the checking range with check-calls and check-raises, especially on boards that equalise ranges or favour the caller. Therefore the in-position player should often bet selectively rather than attack automatically.

The float-bet range is built from value, protection and suitable bluffs while retaining enough showdown value and future continues in check. The wetter or more caller-favourable the board, the more credible and protected the raiser's check range may be. Population tendencies can justify wider stabbing only when opponents actually check-fold too much.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Treat a flop check as a range branch, not proof of weakness.
- Bet more when the checking range is genuinely capped or overfolding; check more against protected ranges.
- Board texture and preflop range interaction determine how often the caller may stab.
- Preserve showdown value and future playability in the check-back range.
- Use branch-specific population evidence before widening the float-bet range.
