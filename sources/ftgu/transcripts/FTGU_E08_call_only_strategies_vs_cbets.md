# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

## Call-only does not mean call everything

A call-only strategy removes raises from the response tree but retains both calls and folds. It is presented as an exception for spots where raising adds little EV or damages the remaining calling range.

## Conditions supporting call-only

The lesson identifies several recurring conditions:

- the defender is in position and can apply pressure later;
- the pot is already large and SPR is low, reducing urgency to build immediately;
- the board is not so wet that immediate protection or value extraction is required;
- multiway participation can improve implied odds for draws and strong made hands;
- the bettor is aggressive enough that keeping bluffs in can outperform raising.

## Range protection and game-tree control

Raising all strong hands can cap the calling range and create unnecessary complexity. Calling preserves strong hands in the passive branch and allows later streets to realise positional or opponent-driven EV.

## Exceptions

Wet boards, vulnerable value and situations where later action is unlikely can restore the need for a raising range. The framework is conditional rather than a general preference for passivity.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- A call-only strategy still folds the bottom of range.
- Position and low SPR reduce the urgency to raise immediately.
- Preserve strong hands in the call branch when raising would cap it.
- Let aggressive opponents continue bluffing when later pressure is likely.
- Multiway participation can increase implied odds for suitable hand classes.
- Wetness and vulnerability can override the call-only default.
