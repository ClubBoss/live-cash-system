# Source-Faithful Record

The lesson rejects automatic c-betting based on "initiative". Out of position, the preflop raiser can have both a positional disadvantage and a range disadvantage on low, wet or middling boards that strongly interact with the caller's concentrated pairs and suited connectors.

On these textures, a very high theoretical checking frequency can be simplified to a range check. The checking range then needs check-calls, check-raises and check-folds. Population opponents may stab too frequently after a check, making the simplification especially effective when the raiser defends more actively than the average pool.

The source limits the concept to out-of-position spots. In position, checking everything would prevent the value region from building the pot, so at least a selective betting range remains necessary.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Preflop initiative does not guarantee postflop range advantage.
- Range-check out of position when the caller owns the board and position compounds the disadvantage.
- Protect the range check with both calls and raises.
- Use check-raises with value and suitable bluffs; check-call stable showdown value and some future bluffs.
- Population over-stabbing can make a high-frequency check simplification outperform its baseline expectation.
- Do not copy the range-check simplification into in-position play.
