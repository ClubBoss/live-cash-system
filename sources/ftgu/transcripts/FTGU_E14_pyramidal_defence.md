# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

## [00:00] Defence as range filtering

The lesson presents a range as a pyramid that narrows from preflop through flop, turn and river. The goal is to avoid both overfolding and calling too much while preserving the strongest and most suitable combinations at each layer.

## [02:10] Start with the full preflop range

A blind-versus-blind example begins with the complete calling range, not the hero's exact hand. The flop then filters the range by made-hand strength, draws, backdoors and board interaction.

## [05:30] Street-by-street contraction

The defender removes the weakest region on the flop, then filters again on the turn and river. The exact fraction is board- and range-dependent. Peter avoids turning the pyramid into one fixed percentage rule.

## [10:50] Minimum defence frequency

MDF is introduced as the minimum portion of a range that must continue to prevent an opponent's bluff from being immediately profitable under the simplified assumptions. Peter says the phrase can be misleading and treats it as a required defence frequency rather than a universal command.

## [13:30] GTO versus exploit

The defence threshold belongs to a baseline game-theory frame. If the opponent underbluffs or overbluffs, the best exploit can deliberately deviate. The player must separate "preventing automatic profit" from "maximising EV against this opponent".

## [15:50] Combo and blocker selection inside the required defence

A synthetic river range demonstrates that, even when a target fraction must call, the combinations chosen should block value and avoid blocking bluffs. The count is performed on the actual surviving range, not on all preflop hands.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Defend a range by filtering it street by street.
- Do not decide river calls from the original preflop range.
- MDF is a baseline constraint, not an exploitative oracle.
- Exact defence depends on board, sizing, range and opponent.
- Use combo and blocker quality to choose which hands occupy the defence quota.
