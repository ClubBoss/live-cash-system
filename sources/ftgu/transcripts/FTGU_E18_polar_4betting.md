# Source-Faithful Record

Polar four-betting places strong value and selected bluffs around a protected calling region. It is preferred against opponents who respond mostly by five-bet jamming or folding, because medium-strength hands retain more EV by calling instead of four-betting and then folding to a shove.

The lesson links more out-of-position four-betting to equity denial and SPR reduction: some hands prefer a smaller four-bet pot over a deeper three-bet pot where the opponent's positional advantage compounds. A value four-bet should not be chosen unless the player is prepared to continue appropriately versus the opponent's five-bet range.

Against weak, underbluffing opponents, the source shifts away from balanced polar bluffs. The response becomes more value-heavy and linear, with many speculative hands calling only for implied odds and marginal stack-off hands removed. Larger value sizing can be used when the opponent flats four-bets too often and rarely uses a jam-or-fold response.
