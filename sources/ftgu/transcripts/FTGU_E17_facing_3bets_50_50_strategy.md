# Source-Faithful Record

The lesson offers continuing roughly half of a sensible opening range as a practical baseline against a standard competent three-bet. It is explicitly described as a ballpark model, not an exact equilibrium law.

The defence can be divided into calls, four-bet value and four-bet bluffs. Pocket pairs, suited hands and dominating broadways tend to retain call EV; dominated offsuit hands and low-nut-potential holdings lose first. The required defence changes with size and position: larger bets and playing out of position justify more folds; undersized bets and position justify materially wider continues.

The source distinguishes point-of-decision EV from whole-hand profit. A call may still lose relative to the start of the hand while outperforming folding after the open is already invested.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Treat repeated three-betting as a range problem, not a demand to defend the current hand emotionally.
- Continue about half only as a safe default against standard size and balanced pressure.
- Adjust defence frequency to size, position, range shape and opponent underbluffing.
- Preserve calls with hands that realise equity, dominate continues or have implied-odds potential.
- Fold more dominated offsuit and low-nut-potential hands.
- Compare a call with folding from the current decision point, not with zero investment.
