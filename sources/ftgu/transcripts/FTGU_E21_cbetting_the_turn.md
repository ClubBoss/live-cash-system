# Source-Faithful Record

The lesson builds turn strategy from the defender's flop response. If the defender used a call-only strategy, the turn range may remain uncapped. If the defender was incentivised to raise strong hands on the flop, the calling branch can be capped on blank turns but becomes uncapped on game-changing cards that create relevant two pair, straights, flushes or strong top-pair regions.

Against a capped range on a blank, the source proposes a multi-size strategy: overbets with strongest value and high-quality bluffs, plus a modest branch for thinner value, protection and weaker bluffs. Against an uncapped range, the source simplifies toward one larger polar size and avoids betting medium-strength hands merely because they were ahead on the flop.

- Use a more selective polar strategy against an uncapped branch.
- Match bluff blockers and value-unblocking properties to the chosen size.
