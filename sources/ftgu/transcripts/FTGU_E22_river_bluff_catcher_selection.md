# Source-Faithful Record

The river decision begins with bet size, required equity and a baseline defence frequency, but the specific calls are selected by actual value/bluff composition and removal effects.

A bluff-catcher should block plausible value and avoid blocking natural bluffs. Hands with identical relative pair strength can have very different call EV because one removes missed draws while another removes sets, two pair or value broadways. Population reads can override the baseline: underbluffing pools justify overfolding, while a genuine overbluff read justifies wider calls.

The instructor repeatedly warns against invented narratives and absolute hand-rank rules. The analysis must use the line, size, ranges, blockers and evidence available.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Derive the baseline call burden from the river size.
- Count plausible value and bluff ancestry before ranking bluff-catchers.
- Prefer hands that block value and preserve bluffs.
- Relative pair rank alone does not determine call quality.
- Use population or player evidence when it is real; do not invent a soul-read.
- A close decision may remain close after analysis; uncertainty is not permission to fabricate evidence.
