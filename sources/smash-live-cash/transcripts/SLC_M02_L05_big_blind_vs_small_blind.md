# Detailed Source-Faithful Outline

## [00:00] Why blind identity changes strategy

The same board can be a high-frequency c-bet versus BB and a heavy check versus SB because the calling ranges are structurally different.

## [02:00] Small-blind calling range

SB enters with a tighter, more condensed range of pocket pairs, suited hands and strong offsuit broadways.

## [04:00] Aggregate report comparison

A multi-flop report contrasts check frequency and sizing versus BB and SB.

## [08:00] Board ranking flips

Boards that are aggressive versus BB can become passive versus SB; 7-6-6 and K-K-J are used as examples.

## [10:00] K-K-J versus BB

The opener has strong range advantage, can c-bet near range and forces difficult pocket-pair and low-card defences.

## [14:00] K-K-J versus SB

SB folds much less and retains stronger pairs and Kx, so the opener checks more and receives fewer automatic folds.

## [16:00] Practical warning

Do not autopilot a familiar c-bet because the board looks favorable; first identify which blind range reached the flop.
