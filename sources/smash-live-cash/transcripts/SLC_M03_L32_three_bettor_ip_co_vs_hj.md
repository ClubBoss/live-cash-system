# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

## High-weight preflop composition

The study uses a 100bb ante configuration with HJ calling a CO 3-bet. The IP 3-bettor retains premiums, AK/AQ, strong suited broadways, suited aces and selected connectors. The OOP caller is heavily weighted toward pocket pairs, suited broadways and selected suited aces, with little JJ+/AK because those hands frequently 4-bet.

## Board ownership follows the range shape

High-card boards usually favour IP. Low and set-rich boards can favour OOP. Ace-high monotone boards may still generate heavy checking because the distributions are polar and blockers matter more than raw equity alone.

## Aggregate-report method

Nick recommends a representative board report with simple small and medium-large sizes. Paired high-card and non-ace monotone boards can approach pure c-betting; low connected and selected ace-high monotone boards require much more checking.

Players who miss the preflop composition often miss high-frequency c-bets. Those bets overperform only when OOP also under-defends or under-check-raises.
