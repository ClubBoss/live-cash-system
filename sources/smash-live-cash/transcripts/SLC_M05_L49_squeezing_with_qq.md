# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

Brad squeezes QQ from the SB after an early-position open and a caller. The opener folds and the caller continues at roughly 100bb effective. On a low 8-4-2-type rainbow flop, Brad imports a prior overbet concept and uses an unnecessarily large bet.

The spot does not require a token size, but a medium bet can build the pot and target pairs without isolating QQ against the strongest range. The squeeze range is structurally polar: overpairs plus a large AK/AQ high-card region. Because it contains so much air, heavy checking can be useful even when the overpairs are strong.

When IP stabs after a check, OOP needs linear value check-raises and high-card bluff raises. If opponents fail to stab enough, betting overpairs more often is sensible; against attentive players, the protected check branch matters more.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Do not copy an overbet from a different range configuration.
- A squeeze range can be overpair-rich and still need heavy checking because of its A-high mass.
- Protect checks with both linear value and bluff raises.
- Choose bet versus check from the opponent’s stab behaviour.
