## [02:30] Put Money In Yourself Against Passive Players

A checking range can be protected in theory, but the exploitative question is whether the opponent will produce the later aggression that makes the slow-play profitable. Against passive or loose-call opponents, direct betting is preferred.

## [05:00] Limping JJ Into a Multiway 3-Bet Pot

Limping creates a difficult out-of-position decision after an isolation raise and 3-bet. Even though the raise is small, JJ may approach a fold because the player is too deep, multiway and poorly positioned.

## [09:30] Nut-Flush Blocker Is Not Universal

A river caller holds top pair with the nut-flush blocker. The blocker is less valuable because the bettor's value range contains sets and two pairs in addition to flushes. Blocking only the nut flush does not meaningfully remove the full value region.

The practical takeaways are to stop limping, avoid giant unnecessary preflop commitments and put value in directly when opponents are unlikely to bluff for you.
