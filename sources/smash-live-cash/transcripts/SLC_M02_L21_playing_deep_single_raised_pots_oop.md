## [23:00] Why Overpairs Must Remain in the Call Line

If the check-call range becomes too ace-high or draw-heavy, IP can value-bet thinly and bluff aggressively on later streets. Strong hands prevent that exploitation.

## [24:55] Closing Principle

Preserve the middle of the range and accept that position deserves an EV advantage on dynamic boards.
