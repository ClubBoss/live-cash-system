# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

Brad 3-bets KTs from CO versus a strong MP opener in an ante game. The action is treated as a reasonable mixed/aggressive expansion rather than a mandatory pure 3-bet.

On a 2-2-2-type flop, the 3-bettor owns premium overpairs but also a large low-equity high-card mass. The distribution is polar. A small c-bet fails to pressure enough of the caller’s pocket-pair and ace-high region; a larger or overbet architecture can better match the value and bluff classes.

After a flop call, the defender is pair- and ace-high-heavy. Hero must rebuild the range and cannot barrel merely because the preflop range was stronger. A polarized line can use geometric turn/river sizes, with bluffs selected to block the strongest continues and preserve intended folds.

Against a player who overfolds underpairs on the turn, overbet turn and give up some rivers. Against a player who overdefends the turn, preserve enough river follow-through.
