# Detailed Source-Faithful Record

## Weighted reports and equity realization

Global averages must reflect how often each flop occurs. An unusual board should not distort the practical baseline merely because it appears once in a study set.

Raw equity is not the same as realization. Middle-equity hands can be forced off later streets, while hands with lower current showdown value can retain more EV through improvement and future nut potential.

## Protect the check-back range

On low or paired boards, IP checks some suited broadways, connected hands and medium-strength made hands because they can improve, call future overbets and prevent the check-back branch from becoming capped.

Pocket pairs can outperform top pair as bluff-catchers when they avoid blocking natural bluffs and retain stronger future improvement. Current hand rank alone is insufficient.

## Verified tail: bluff ancestry

The rerun confirms that obvious barrel candidates should not all be spent on the flop. Selected low-equity turn barrels are needed so blank and completed-draw rivers retain credible bluffs.

Missed draws and no-showdown hands are selected by which value hands they block and which bluff-catchers they leave available. A natural-looking draw can be a poor bluff when it removes the folds the bet targets.

# Explicit Instructor Mechanisms

- Weight aggregate findings by board occurrence.
- Protect check-back ranges with hands that can absorb future pressure.
- Judge bluff-catchers by blockers, ancestry and future improvement.
- Seed river bluffs on the turn; equity-driven draws alone are often insufficient.
