# Detailed Transcript

## [00:00] The Multiway Sandwich as the Central Dynamic

Nick introduces the sandwich effect: the player between two live ranges must proceed carefully, while the player closing the action can use position and shared defence to apply pressure. He emphasises that multiway poker is structurally different from heads-up poker because one player does not carry the full defensive burden.

## [02:00] Why Small Sizes Become More Powerful Multiway

As additional players enter, small bets can target stronger hands because every defender must account for action behind. The same ideas become more pronounced four-, five- or six-way, although the lesson models a three-way pot.

## [03:20] Triton Setup: CO, BTN and BB on T-9-7 Rainbow

The example is approximately 100bb in an ante game. CO opens, BTN calls and BB defends. All three ranges interact meaningfully with T-9-7 rainbow, but BTN has ultimate position and the initial opener is constrained by two uncapped ranges.

## [05:00] The Initial Opener Should Check Very Often

CO checks close to the entire range in the displayed baseline. Rather than betting blindly into two players, the opener can wait for BTN to bet and BB to act, then use calls and selective check-raises with much greater visibility.

## [08:00] BTN Aggression and BB's Sandwich Problem

After checks, BTN may bet aggressively because CO has shown passivity. BB cannot freely check-raise top pairs and draws because CO remains behind with a range that contains straights, sets and strong pair-plus-draw hands.

## [12:00] Shared Defence and Selective Check-Raising

The BB's raises must be tighter and more equity- and blocker-driven than heads-up. The opener, after BTN bets and BB calls, can use a linear check-raise range to punish BTN and protect vulnerable made hands.

## [17:00] Bluff Categories Become Tighter Multiway

Nick rejects random low-equity “savage” bluffs in this structure. Multiway bluffs should generally have equity plus removal: pair-plus-draw hands, gutters with blockers and hands that match the value region.

## [22:00] Preserve the Strategic Shape When Exploiting

If opponents overfold, increase the frequency of the already-correct bluff candidates rather than introducing hands with no relevant properties. More players must be cleared, so abandoning the correct shape creates costly collisions with strong ranges.

## [25:00] The Opener's Linear Check-Raise

After BTN bets and BB calls, CO can pure or heavily raise strong value and use pair-plus-gutter hands as linear protection raises. The goal is to deny BTN's positional realisation and avoid giving both opponents another card cheaply.

## [29:00] Backup Equity Matters More Multiway

Top pair without backup can become a pure fold against a strong call plus check-raise sequence. Deep and multiway pots both increase the value of backup equity and nutted improvement paths.
