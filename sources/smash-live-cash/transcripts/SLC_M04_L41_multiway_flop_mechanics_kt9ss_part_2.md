## [03:00] Fast-Play More Value Against Passive Closing Players

Sets, straights and two pair can bet or raise more directly when BB is unlikely to build the pot. Waiting for aggression that will not arrive causes strong hands to under-realise.

## [07:00] The Solver Pulls Bluffs From Apparently Irrelevant Hands

On later streets, IP uses low cards and pocket pairs that unblock folds and block continues. Visible draw strength is not the only criterion; removal against the call-call range selects many barrels.

## [11:00] Pot, Two-Thirds and One-Third Turn Abstractions

The available turn sizes represent different pressure levels. Low-equity hands often choose the larger size when they remove the defender's strongest continues and have little showdown value.

## [15:00] Population Give-Ups Change Slow-Play EV

Equilibrium assumes IP and BB will continue applying pressure with difficult bluff candidates. When live players check back or give up those hands, passive value lines lose money and direct betting improves.

## [19:00] Construct Barrels Against the Call-Call Range

The correct question is which hands unblock the folds created by two calls and which cards block the surviving value. Random low hands can become good bluffs because they do not interfere with the hands being targeted.

## [23:00] Practical Multiway Adjustment

Do not copy a slow-play frequency built on aggression that the actual table lacks. Preserve the value/bluff shape, but move value forward and select barrels according to the real population response.
