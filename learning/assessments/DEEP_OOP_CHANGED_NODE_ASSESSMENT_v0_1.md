# Deep OOP Changed-Node Assessment v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE_ORIGINAL_ASSESSMENT`

## Competencies

1. calculate post-action SPR;
2. ignore nominal depth labels;
3. classify robust, volatile and frail;
4. distinguish merged from polar bets;
5. identify top-end raise support;
6. separate call and raise thresholds;
7. reject discomfort raises;
8. adjust to credible versus missing future aggression.

## Assessment families

### A — Geometry

Hold cards and board constant; change pot size, bet size, stack behind or straddle denominator.

### B — Hand resilience

Hold node constant; change clean redraw, dominated redraw, kicker blocking air or backup nut path.

### C — Range shape

Hold size constant; change range-wide bettor, selective bettor, under-raiser or over-barreller.

### D — Board urgency

Hold ranges constant; change paired static, disconnected static, connected two-tone or ownership-restoring turn.

### E — Opponent future action

Hold theory node constant; change credible barrels, passive give-up, unknown or single anecdote.

## Pass standard

The action and reason must both update under the changed variable.

Automatic failure:

- “deep means call”;
- “big bet means fold”;
- “raise to make the hand easier”;
- “top pair is always value”;
- exact solver frequency without evidence.
