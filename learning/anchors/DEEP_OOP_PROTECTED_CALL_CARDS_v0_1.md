# Deep OOP Protected-Call Cards v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE_TABLE_FACING_DIRECTIONAL_CARDS`

## D1 — Geometry before depth label

```text
post-call SPR = (stack behind - call) / (pot + 2 x call)
```

Use:

- `C <=1.5` compressed;
- `M 1.5–4` middle;
- `E >4` extended.

Cue:

> How many meaningful bets remain after I call?

## D2 — Classify R / V / F

### R — robust

Beats bluffs now, survives many turns, has clean redraws or nut paths, and can resist later pressure.

### V — volatile

Often ahead now but loses equity on many turns and may gain real value from denial.

### F — frail

Can lose to some bluffs, improves dirty, and cannot withstand future pressure.

Cue:

> Does this hand survive the tree, or only the flop?

## D3 — Read the bet shape

```text
small + wide/merged
→ call wider
→ protection raises can widen

large + selective/polar
→ calls compress
→ thin raises disappear
```

Cue:

> What does this player bet with at this size?

## D4 — Protect the call branch

Strong calls are not missed value when they:

- keep air in;
- protect weaker calls;
- preserve future check-raises/leads;
- avoid isolating against strength;
- retain clean future decisions.

Cue:

> Which strong hands stop my calls from becoming face-up?

## D5 — Raise for a job, never relief

Valid jobs:

- value versus continues;
- denial against live equity;
- equity plus fold pressure;
- branch protection.

Invalid jobs:

- avoid a turn;
- take control;
- the pot feels big;
- probably ahead;
- “we are deep.”

Cue:

> What better outcome does raising create against the continuing range?

## Opponent switch

- credible future aggressor → preserve more robust calls;
- passive future actor → move more value forward, using baseline-approved candidates.

## 100 / 200 / 400 reminder

- 100bb SRP can still be extended;
- 200bb magnifies position and runout mistakes;
- 400bb with a straddle or large preflop pot may not be operationally deep;
- recalculate; do not label.

## Five-second sequence

```text
SPR → R/V/F → bet shape → top-end → future aggression → action
```

Directional execution and drills only. Not an exact solver matrix or universal combo rule.
