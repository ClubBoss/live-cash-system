## Session-level priorities

1. Preserve positional discipline as stacks deepen.
2. Do not allow tournament familiarity to hide cash-specific rake, rebuy, and reverse-implied-odds effects.
3. Treat OOP realisation and future-street pressure as first-class costs.
4. Use observed live tendencies to deviate from baseline, but distinguish evidence from assumption.
5. Prefer a small stable strategy over untrained solver mixtures.
