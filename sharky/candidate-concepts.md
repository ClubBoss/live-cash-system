## Admission guard

A course idea is not a product requirement by default. Record it here only when it offers one or more of:

- a clearer mental model;
- a high-frequency decision drill;
- useful error diagnosis;
- improved transfer from explanation to play;
- lower cognitive load without material strategy loss.

Statuses:

- `OBSERVED`
- `ANALYSIS_REQUIRED`
- `PROMISING`
- `VALIDATED`
- `DORMANT`
- `REJECTED`
